<h1 align="center">Annie The Blacksmith</h1>

&emsp;**Annie** is the local Silverhaven blacksmith located within the forge at spawn. She is one of the mission giver NPCs and can be quickly teleported to by using the /warp forge command.



## Purpose
&emsp;Annie is one of the giftable NPCs in Otter and is the vendor responsible for repairing the durability on items. Players are able to click on Annie to access her forge to restore an item to full durability at the cost of repair runes and a small amount of money. You can also quickly access her forge menu with the /forge command.

&emsp;Her missions, while not the easiest, are still relatively quick to complete compared to the others on the list. You can't click on her to access them, unlike most of the other NPCs, but they can be found on page 2 of the mission menu with the /mission command. They consist of breaking a certain number of ore blocks, repairing an item at her forge, using an anvil, or crafting items. 

&emsp;Annie's gifts, while slightly on the more expensive side, are normally composed of raw ore and colored blocks. Offering her the gift will give you a unique response in chat and a redeemable version of said gift. Gifts from our favorite blacksmith consist of the standard gift items along with repair runes, mines furniture, armor, and a rare item exclusive to the gift you gave her. The only exception to this are the Candy Pail gifts that all give you the same items when redeemed. 


## Description

&emsp;Annie is a female NPC that can be found within the forge building located inside of spawn. You can quickly teleport to it by using the /warp forge command if you have trouble finding it.  

Annie has long maroon hair, the majority of which falls freely behind her while the rest is done up in two twin braids that hang on the front of her skin. Her outfit is composed of a custom set of armor that consists of a chest plate, gauntlets, greaves, and boots.


## Trivia

- She is 6 feet tall
- Annie carries a hammer in her offhand and wears a pair of googles, both of which can only be seen on her person
